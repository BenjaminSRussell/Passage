## Description

**Goal.** Quality orders are generated by rules at receipt, production and shipment; results drive disposition, nonconformance, corrective action and supplier scorecards.

**Phase intent.** Advanced warehouse management, mobile execution, quality management, transportation and B2B/EDI/e-invoicing integration.

**Replaces.**
- AX / D365: Quality management: test groups, quality associations, quality orders, nonconformances, corrections, certificates of analysis, inventory blocking
- SAP: QM: inspection plans, inspection lots (QA01), results recording, usage decisions, quality notifications (QM01), CAPA, vendor evaluation

**Owned paths (hard boundary — create and edit only here).**
- services/quality/
- web modules: apps/web/src/modules/quality/

**Deliverables.**
1. Tests, test groups, sampling plans (AQL), quality associations (when/what triggers a quality order)
2. Quality orders with results recording (variable/attribute), usage decision, automatic inventory blocking/release
3. Nonconformance → correction/CAPA workflow with root cause (8D template), cost of quality
4. Certificates of analysis, supplier quality scorecards, SPC charts from results
5. Device/instrument calibration tracking
6. Product compliance: regulated/restricted substance lists with BOM roll-up, and safety data sheets with distribution on shipment
7. Incoming vendor certificates gating acceptance, and source inspection before delivery
8. Physical sample management (retention samples), multiple specification sets, in-process inspection points
9. Stability studies with scheduled pull points, audit management, and FMEA/control plans (AIAG-VDA)

**Deploy.** Quality workspace enabled for the demo plants; CoA generation on shipments.

**Detailed requirements.** docs/prd/phase-6-logistics.md, section for Wave 33 (functional requirements, data model, APIs/events, business rules, acceptance criteria, test plan). Engineering rules: docs/engineering/CONVENTIONS.md. Stack: docs/adr/0001-platform-stack.md. Platform kernel to build on: platform/go.
