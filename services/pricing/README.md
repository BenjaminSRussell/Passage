# pricing (wave 26, PSG-26)

Deterministic pricing engine for Passage: resolves net prices and discounts for a
customer / product / date / quantity / currency tuple from condition records, and returns
the ordered trace of conditions that produced the price. Ticket PSG-26, PRD §5.26.

Built in slice 6:

- **Vendor rebate agreements** (FR-26.15, BR-26.11, AC-26.11): `partyRole` VENDOR on a rebate
  agreement with `vendorId` (and no customer); accounts default to 2160 vendor rebate receivable /
  5100 purchase rebate income. `POST /rebates/vendor-accruals` stands in for the w19
  vendor-invoice.posted consumer (vendor-invoiced net or received quantity, same period lock and
  tier recompute as customer accruals) and writes `rebateaccrual.posted` with `party_role` VENDOR
  and Dr 2160 / Cr 5100. The two invoice feeds never cross (422 PRC-0045).
- **Vendor rebate claims**: `GET /rebates/agreements/{id}/claim-preview?period=` shows what a period
  would claim; `POST /rebates/vendor-claims` creates claim `RC-000001`, `RC-000002`, ... (per legal
  entity) for the period's accruals less its earlier claims, under the period lock, OPEN, with
  `vendorrebateclaim.created`. Nothing accrued is 422 PRC-0043; a fully claimed period is 409
  PRC-0015; an over-claimed period produces a negative claim settled by VENDOR_INVOICE; otherwise
  the claim settles by CREDIT_NOTE (default) or PAYMENT_DEDUCTION. `:approve` (duty
  `pricing.rebate-claim.approve`, versioned, `Idempotency-Key`) refuses the claim's creator (403
  PRC-0046) and writes `vendorrebateclaim.approved` for w19 AP. The Vendor rebate claims inquiry
  filters by agreement, vendor, period and status.
- **Broker contracts and royalty agreements** (FR-26.16, FR-26.17): `/broker-contracts` pays a broker
  vendor a percentage of line net or an amount per unit on the invoices of its customer and product
  scope; `/royalty-agreements` pays a licensor vendor tiered rates (the rebate tier rule, BR-26.8) on
  one product or product group with a `minimumRoyalty` per period. `POST .../{id}/accruals` stands in
  for the `invoice.posted` consumer under the service-only duty `pricing.contract.accrue`: a line
  accrues once, a broker credit note line reverses the original in proportion and a royalty credit
  lowers the period basis (422 PRC-0059 beyond the original line, also after the contract closed), and `contractaccrual.posted` carries Dr 6210 / Cr
  2175 (broker) or Dr 6220 / Cr 2176 (royalty). `POST /royalty-agreements/{id}:close-period` posts the
  minimum royalty shortfall once (PRC-0017 notice) and locks the period (409 PRC-0060). `POST
  /contract-claims` claims a period as `BC-n` or `RY-n` (a royalty period must be closed, 409
  PRC-0062), `:approve` refuses the creator (403 PRC-0046) and writes `contractclaim.approved` for AP.
- **Record snapshot cache** (FR-26.14): `store.Cache` keeps the ACTIVE record set of a resolve per
  tenant, caller scope, legal entity, currency, customer group and pricing day for
  `PRICING_CACHE_TTL` (default 60s, 10s to 600s, 0 turns it off); activate, withdraw, journal post
  and reverse, and campaign kill and restore empty it on this replica.
- Migration `0006_vendor_rebates.sql` (party_role and vendor_id with a named party check,
  vendor_rebate_claim with RLS and legal-entity scope).
- **Web**: customer or vendor type on the rebate form (accounts follow the type), type filter on the
  rebate list, the claims card on a vendor agreement (preview a period, claim it, list its claims),
  the Vendor rebate claims inquiry and the claim detail with approval.

Built in slice 5:

- **Campaigns** (FR-26.10): a campaign has a name, validity, an optional target customer group,
  a budget and currency, and a kill switch. A condition record joins a campaign through
  `campaignId` (same legal entity; 404 PRC-0041 when unknown); the campaign is part of the record
  key, so a campaign record does not overlap the plain record of the same key, and at one access
  it outranks the plain record (BR-26.1 step 4: most dimensions matched). Resolve keeps a campaign
  record only while the campaign is live (not killed, pricing date inside its validity) and, for a
  targeted campaign, only when the request's `customerGroupId` is the target group. `:kill` and
  `:restore` (versioned, `Idempotency-Key`) take effect on the next resolve
  (`campaign_kill_switch_effect` NEXT_RESOLVE) and write `campaign.killed` / `campaign.restored`.
  `:revise` (versioned, `Idempotency-Key`) changes the name, validity, target group or budget of a
  live or killed campaign (the currency never changes) and writes `campaign.revised`; it never
  changes how a day before today priced (a started campaign keeps its start and target group).
  The budget is recorded, not enforced (see NOT-IMPLEMENTED.md).
- **Pricing procedures** (FR-26.1): versioned procedures per legal entity and name (AX_STANDARD,
  SAP_RVAA01, ...). Each version holds steps 10–990 in increasing order with exactly one PRICE
  step, the condition type (the five the engine prices plus FREIGHT, REBATE_ACCRUAL, ROUNDING and
  FREE_GOODS; each type at most once, since records are keyed by type), an optional CEL requirement (≤ 512 characters), an exclusion group with its mode
  (BEST or FIRST, one mode per group), the statistical flag and the account key. Create numbers
  the next version under an advisory lock; `:revise` replaces a DRAFT's steps at its `revision`;
  `:activate` supersedes the ACTIVE version of the same name in one transaction and writes
  `procedure.activated`. ACTIVE and SUPERSEDED versions are immutable (409 PRC-0008). Writes need
  `pricing.admin` (PricingAdmin). Slice 14 makes the
  engine run the ACTIVE procedure: `pricing_procedure_assignment` (migration `0014`) names the
  procedure per legal entity and sales channel (an empty channel is the default), `resolve` and
  `simulate` accept `salesChannel` and `procedure`, and the result names the procedure version
  (part of the explain reference). Steps run in step order; one member of each exclusion group
  survives per line (BEST lowest net, FIRST lowest step; losers stay in the trace with the
  reason); a statistical step is reported with its amount and account key and leaves the net
  alone. Every applied condition, PRICE included, carries its step number and account key.
  Without an assignment the reference order runs. Step CEL requirements are stored but
  not evaluated (NOT-IMPLEMENTED.md). Web: Procedure determination.
- Migration `0005_procedures_campaigns.sql` (campaign, pricing_procedure, condition_record.campaign_id
  with a tenant-scoped foreign key; RLS and legal-entity scope on both new tables).
- **Web**: campaign list, create and detail (kill and restore); procedure designer with the version
  list, a step editor for new versions and DRAFT revisions, and activation; the trade agreement
  form takes an optional campaign.

Built in slice 4:

- **Trade promotion funds** (FR-26.19, BR-26.15): funds with type, currency, customer scope, owner,
  budget per period (YYYY-MM) and `allowOverspend`. `:attribute` charges usage (campaign accrual,
  rebate accrual, claim, deduction) to a period under the fund row lock: 422 PRC-0012 when usage
  would exceed the period budget without overspend, 409 PRC-0011 when the fund is closed. Each
  attribution writes `fundusage.recorded` in the same transaction; a repeated `Idempotency-Key`
  replays the first response without adding usage. `:revise` changes the budget and overspend flag
  at the body `version` (the budget must still cover every period's usage without overspend);
  `:close` freezes usage and `:reopen` lifts it (both versioned, `fund.closed` / `fund.reopened`).
  `GET /funds/{id}/usage` is the Fund usage inquiry: usage per period and source plus budget, used
  and remaining per period.
- **Promotion planning** (FR-26.20): a plan names the merchandising event category and type,
  customer scope, optional fund (same legal entity and currency, open), one product, dates, the 13
  weekly units before the promotion, forecast lift % and planned spend. The response derives the
  baseline (13-week average), forecast units (baseline × started weeks × (1 + lift %)) and, once
  `:actuals` records the units, weeks and spend in the window, the effectiveness
  (actual weekly average − baseline) / baseline in percent.
- Migration `0004_funds_promotions.sql` (trade_fund, trade_fund_usage, promotion_plan; RLS and
  legal-entity scope on each).
- **Web**: fund list, create and detail (attribute usage, revise budget, close and reopen, usage by
  period and source); promotion list, create and detail (baseline, forecast, actuals and
  effectiveness).

Built in slice 3:

- **Commission transactions and posting** (FR-26.12, FR-26.18, BR-26.12, AC-26.12): sales groups
  with reps and share percentages (summing to 100, kept in entered order so the first rep takes the
  rounding residual), commission rules per sales group and customer/item commission group (one per
  combination) resolved specific to general (PRC-0013 when none matches), REVENUE or MARGIN basis
  (MARGIN needs the line's `costSnapshot`). `POST /commission/postings` stands in for the
  invoice.posted consumer: one transaction per rep (Dr 6200 / Cr 2170) with
  `commission.calculated` in the same transaction; a redelivered line returns the stored rows. A
  credit note line reverses with the original line's basis, rate and shares, so a full credit nets
  every rep to zero even after the rule changed; credits are capped at the invoiced net (PRC-0037).
  Commission inquiry by rep, sales group, month, customer and invoice line. Migration
  `0003_commission_retro.sql` (sales_group, sales_group_rep, commission_rule,
  commission_transaction; RLS on each).
- **Retroactive rebate recompute** (FR-26.23, BR-26.7, BR-26.14, AC-26.14): an agreement is active
  from its creation day; when its `validFrom` is earlier, `POST /rebates/agreements/{id}:retro-recompute`
  replays the invoices posted between `validFrom` and the activation (the caller supplies them until
  the w29/w30 invoice ledger exists), adds the lines not accrued before to their period's basis, and
  posts the delta per period with `retroactive=true` (one accrual row and one `rebateaccrual.posted`
  event per period). Posted invoices are never re-priced; the `Idempotency-Key` names the run, and a
  second run over the same invoices posts nothing. The response carries the PRC-0006 notice.
- **Web**: sales group list, create (reps and shares) and detail (rules and adding a rule), the
  commission transactions inquiry, and the retroactive recompute form on the rebate agreement detail
  (the accruals grid marks retroactive rows).

Built in slice 2:

- **Multiline discounts and surcharges** (FR-26.5, FR-26.6, BR-26.4): `MULTILINE_DISC` records
  (keyed by product group or all products) match on the summed quantity of the lines sharing a
  product group and unit; `SURCHARGE` records (order level) apply when the subtotal is below the
  small-order threshold (250 USD, 200 EUR) and are reported as `surcharge` as well as added to
  `orderNet`. Migration `0002_journals_rebates.sql` widens the condition type check.
- **Price journals** (FR-26.9, AC-26.7, AC-26.10): `internal/domain/journal.go` validates a journal
  as a whole (rules, stale versions, overlaps with active records and with other lines, one line per
  record) and builds the reversal; `internal/store/journal.go` posts it in one transaction under the
  legal entity's journal lock (activations take the same lock in shared mode), writes one
  activated/withdrawn event per record plus `journal.posted`, and reverses into a new journal.
  Post and reverse replay on a repeated `Idempotency-Key`.
- **Rebate agreements and accruals** (FR-26.11, BR-26.8, AC-26.8): tiered agreements by customer or
  customer group; `domain.Accrue` moves a period to its target at the new cumulative basis and books
  the difference on the invoice line, so a tier crossing recomputes the period. The store serialises
  accruals per (agreement, period), ignores a redelivered invoice line and writes
  `rebateaccrual.posted` (Dr 4150 / Cr 2150 by default) for the ledger.
- **Web**: price journal list, create and detail (validate, post, reverse, before/after per line);
  rebate agreement list, create (tiers) and detail (accruals by period); journals-to-post tile.

```
internal/api/           HTTP handlers and validation
internal/events/        event type constants and payloads
migrations/             0001_init.sql (condition_record, outbox, idempotency_keys), 0002 (journals, rebates), 0003 (commission, retro flag), 0004 (funds, promotions), 0005 (procedures, campaigns), 0006 (vendor rebates and claims), 0014 (procedure determination); all with RLS

charts/pricing/         Helm chart
testdata/               fixtures
```

## Configuration

12-factor env vars, validated at startup: `PASSAGE_ENV`, `SERVICE_NAME`, `HTTP_ADDR`,
`ADMIN_ADDR`, `DATABASE_URL`, `PRICING_MAX_LINES` (500), `PRICING_EXPLAIN_MAX_BYTES` (65536).
`PRICING_CACHE_TTL` (60s; a duration or whole seconds, 10s to 600s, 0 turns the record snapshot
cache off). Optional replica cache consumer (chart `cacheConsumer`): `KAFKA_BROKERS` starts it
when the service principal `pricing-cache` holds `pricing.cache.invalidate`; `KAFKA_USERNAME`,
`KAFKA_PASSWORD` (or `KAFKA_PASSWORD_FILE`) and `KAFKA_TLS_CA_FILE` as for every consumer. Each
replica reads under the group `pricing-cache-<HOSTNAME>`; `KAFKA_GROUP` is ignored.
