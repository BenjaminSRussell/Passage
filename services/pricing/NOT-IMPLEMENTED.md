| Broker accrual period | decision noted | A broker contract accrues and is claimed by calendar month (PRD §5.26.4 gives a broker contract no period field); a royalty agreement uses its own MONTH, QUARTER or YEAR. A credit note line lands in the period of its own date, so a broker claim for that period can be negative; AP offsets it against the vendor's next payment. |
| Broker and royalty credit note lines | decision noted | A credit note line reverses an accrual the contract already took, so it needs only the contract currency: it is taken after the contract closed or its validity ended (an invoice line then is PRC-0061 or 422), otherwise the broker or licensor would keep the commission on a credited sale. It lands in the period of its own date (PRC-0060 when that royalty period is closed). A broker credit reverses the original accrual in proportion. A royalty credit lowers the cumulative basis of its own period and the tier rule books the delta (as rebate credits do, BR-26.8), capped at the net sales or quantity still creditable on the original line (PRC-0059); so a credit dated in a later period with no sales of its own reverses nothing, and the original period is not reopened. Reversing into the original period (and into the first open period when that one is closed, as BR-26.14 does for retro deltas) is a product decision. |
| Royalty period close timing | decision noted | `:close-period` closes any MONTH, QUARTER or YEAR period of the agreement that overlaps its validity, also before the period has ended; invoice lines dated in a closed period are then PRC-0060. The PRD says only that the shortfall posts "at period close"; whether a close before the last day (or before the w14 fiscal period close) is refused is a product decision, and the period-end job will close each period once it has ended. The minimum is not prorated when the validity covers only part of a period. |
