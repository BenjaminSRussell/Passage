# Not implemented in PSG-13

The service ships the tax engine, provider failover with a recommit queue, settlement reconciliation to the general ledger, a SAF-T tax extract, the stateless calculate and withholding endpoints, the store, contracts, Dockerfile and Helm chart. The web module stays a navigation stub.

| Deliverable or AC | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| AC1: tax matrix of 500 scenarios | partial | `TestMatrix500` runs 500 scenarios from `testdata/matrix500.json` (US 200, EU 180, IN 70, CA 50; state+county+city stacks, IN IGST/CESS, effective-dated rate history, reverse charge) against an exact-rational oracle; bracket, cap, exemption and certificate cases are asserted in `TestCalculate_Table` and `TestCalculate_Mechanisms`. OPEN ITEM: community sourcing and accountant sign-off are not done; the named accountant is an open architecture item |
| AC2: settlement reconciles to the GL tax accounts | built | `POST /v1/tax/settlements:run` reads each tax account's movement from the ledger (`internal/ledger`, `platform/ledgerclient` with the `tax` service grant of `ledger.balance.read`) and `Settle` refuses any difference with TAX-0008. Proven against `ledgerclient.Fake` in `internal/ledger` and through the router and the real store in `TestSettleThroughTheRouterReconcilesToTheLedger`; `internal/store` has the DB tests. Limits: the period must be whole calendar months, the `code\|direction` to account map and ledger are stored per legal entity (`PUT /v1/tax/ledger-config`), never read from the settlement request, transactions are recorded by callers through `POST /v1/tax/transactions` (calculate stays stateless), amounts are assumed to be in the ledger's accounting currency, and the settlement's own ledger posting (`Poster`, key `post:tax:settlement:<id>:settle`) is still not wired because the clearing accounts are unspecified |
| AC3: provider failover | built | `CalculateWithFailover` falls back within 2 s and flags the document (TAX-0007); the fallback now writes `tax_recommit_queue` and `dev.passage.tax.provider.fallback.v1` through the outbox in one transaction, and `POST /v1/tax/recommit:run` drains the queue. Limits: no Avalara or Vertex adapter exists (the provider map is empty in `cmd/tax`, they need Vault credentials), so the path is exercised with test providers; recommit stores the recalculated result but does not call the provider's `Commit`; nothing calls the recommit route on a schedule yet |
| AC4: SAF-T export validates against the OECD XSD | partial | `GET /v1/tax/saft` exports header and tax table in the OECD 2.00 namespace; `saft.Input` also carries Customers, Suppliers and GeneralLedgerEntries journals, and `Build` runs `CheckDocument` on its own output (required fields, unique ids, one positive debit or credit per line, at least two lines, period matches date and selection period, balanced transactions, `NumberOfEntries`/`TotalDebit`/`TotalCredit` control totals, customer and supplier references). Tests: `TestBuild_RefusesInvalidSections`, `TestCheckDocument_RejectsBrokenControlTotalsAndStructure`, `TestExport_LedgerAndPartiesValidateAgainstTheSchema`, `TestSchemaRejectsBrokenLedgerSections`; the XSD subset in `testdata/saft/` covers the three sections too. OPEN ITEMS: validating against the official OECD XSD needs it fetched by hand (oecd.org answers 403 to automated downloads, steps in `testdata/saft/SOURCE.md`); `GET /v1/tax/saft?ledger_id=` reads the period's journal lines from the ledger (`ledgerclient.JournalLines`, duty `ledger.entry.lookup`, ledger pinned to the caller's legal entity through `ledger.ledger.lookup`; the `tax` service grant gained exactly those two read duties) and emits them as GeneralLedgerEntries, proven end to end with `ledgerclient.Fake` in `TestSAFTExportEmitsTheLedgerJournalsOfTheEntityLedger` (output passes `CheckDocument` and `xmllint`), `TestSAFTExportRefusesALedgerOfAnotherEntityAndBadInput` and `TestSAFTExportRefusesAnUnbalancedLedgerAndSurfacesLedgerOutages`; Customers and Suppliers are NOT filled by the route: a ledger line carries no customer or vendor reference (dimension values are 20 characters, the party service ids are UUIDs) and the party service stores and serves no tax id, so there is nothing truthful to read, and no party grant was added; only callers of `saft.Build` supply them. Without `ledger_id` the route still exports header and tax table only; accountant review stays human; the VAT return, ESL and Intrastat extracts are not built |
| AC6: contracts and chart | built | `api/openapi.yaml` documents every route with schemas and duties and `TestOpenAPIMatchesRoutesAndDuties` checks routes, duties and the policy seed; `events/asyncapi.yaml` is checked by `TestEventsMatchAsyncAPI`; `Dockerfile` and `charts/tax` (helm lint) follow the expense service |
