# Not implemented in PSG-13

The service ships the tax engine, provider failover with a recommit queue, settlement reconciliation to the general ledger, a SAF-T tax extract, the stateless calculate and withholding endpoints, the store, contracts, Dockerfile and Helm chart. The web module is the tax configuration workspace (setup, VAT return, EU sales list, Intrastat).

OPEN ITEM: community sourcing and accountant sign-off are not done; the named accountant is an open architecture item |
| AC2: settlement reconciles to the GL tax accounts | built | `POST /v1/tax/settlements:run` reads each tax account's movement from the ledger (`internal/ledger`, `platform/ledgerclient` with the `tax` service grant of `ledger.balance.read`) and `Settle` refuses any difference with TAX-0008. Proven against `ledgerclient.Fake` in `internal/ledger` and through the router and the real store in `TestSettleThroughTheRouterReconcilesToTheLedger`; `internal/store` has the DB tests. | AC4: SAF-T export validates against the OECD XSD | partial | `GET /v1/tax/saft` exports header and tax table in the OECD 2.00 namespace; `saft.Input` also carries Customers, Suppliers and GeneralLedgerEntries journals, and `Build` runs `CheckDocument` on its own output (required fields, unique ids, one positive debit or credit per line, at least two lines, period matches date and selection period, balanced transactions, `NumberOfEntries`/`TotalDebit`/`TotalCredit` control totals, customer and supplier references). Tests: `TestBuild_RefusesInvalidSections`, `TestCheckDocument_RejectsBrokenControlTotalsAndStructure`, `TestExport_LedgerAndPartiesValidateAgainstTheSchema`, `TestSchemaRejectsBrokenLedgerSections`; the XSD subset in `testdata/saft/` covers the three sections too. OPEN ITEMS: validating against the official OECD XSD needs it fetched by hand (oecd.org answers 403 to automated downloads, steps in `testdata/saft/SOURCE.md`); `GET /v1/tax/saft?ledger_id=` reads the ledger (`ledgerclient` `Ledger`, `Accounts`, `Balances`, `JournalLines`; the `tax` service grant is `ledger.balance.read`, `ledger.entry.lookup`, `ledger.ledger.lookup` and `ledger.account.lookup`, all reads), pins the ledger to the caller's legal entity, refuses a `currency` that differs from the ledger's accounting currency (422 TAX-0011) and a period that is not whole calendar months (400), and emits GeneralLedgerAccounts (chart with opening and closing balances), then GeneralLedgerEntries with AmountStructure amounts. A document that fails `CheckDocument` on ledger data is 502 TAX-0012, one that fails without ledger data would be 500; error details name sections and positions, never ids or amounts. Proven end to end with `ledgerclient.Fake` in `TestSAFTExportEmitsTheLedgerJournalsOfTheEntityLedger` (passes `CheckDocument` and `xmllint`), `TestSAFTExportRefusesALedgerOfAnotherEntityAndBadInput`, `TestSAFTExportRefusesAnUnbalancedLedgerAndSurfacesLedgerOutages` and `TestCheckDocument_ErrorsNameNoLedgerValues`. Limits: Period and PeriodYear are the calendar month and year of the posting date because ledger lines carry no fiscal period (the checker accepts any Period 1 to 12 and a PeriodYear within a year of the date); SystemEntryDate is the posting date at 00:00:00 because lines carry no entry timestamp; a journal line count above `ledgerclient.MaxJournalLines` fails the export instead of growing memory; Customers and Suppliers are NOT filled by the route: a ledger line carries no customer or vendor reference (dimension values are 20 characters, the party service ids are UUIDs) and the party service stores and serves no tax id, so no party grant was added and only callers of `saft.Build` supply them. Without `ledger_id` the route still exports header and tax table only; accountant review stays human; the VAT return, ESL and Intrastat extracts are not built |

| Deliverable 6: Intrastat master data and journal | built except the live feed | master data of six kinds, journal with transfer, validation, compression, correction and CSV export. Since FIN-A: seeded standard lists (`POST /v1/tax/intrastat/master:seed`: transaction codes, transport modes, delivery terms, countries, ports and a starter set of CN8 codes; the full CN table loads through `master:import`, up to 5000 rows a call), and a feed: `POST /v1/tax/intrastat/movements` stages shipments and receipts (duty `tax.intrastat.feed`), `/intrastat/products/{id}` maps a product to its commodity code, origin and mass, and `POST /journals/{id}/transfer-movements` fills a draft journal from the staged movements of its flow and month (movements with an unmapped product stay staged and are named). NOT built: fulfillment and receiving calling the movements route or a consumer of their events. Their events carry no partner country, commodity code, origin or weight (the product and party masters hold none), so there is nothing yet to feed from; no national file format; weights and values are not converted nor thresholds applied. The seeded lists are a starting point to be checked against the current regulation (#512) |
| Deliverables 7, 8: VAT recovery policies, withholding remittance runs | built | Recovery: `/v1/tax/recovery-policies` (per code and item tax group, effective-dated, non-overlapping) feed the engine's recoverable share of an input tax (migration `0006`), input transactions may record their `recoverable_amount`, and `GET /v1/tax/vat-reclaim` totals the input tax of a period per code into what is reclaimed and what stays in cost (a transaction without a recoverable amount takes the code's own `recoverable_pct` and is counted as estimated).
