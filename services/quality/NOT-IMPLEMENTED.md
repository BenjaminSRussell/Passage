# NOT-IMPLEMENTED: quality (PSG-33, wave 33)

Slice 1 builds the sampling, association, test-group, instrument, quality-order,
result and usage-decision core, with web screens for each. Slice 2 adds product
compliance (versioned lists, item compositions, BOM roll-up and versioned evaluation
results), stability studies with their generated pull schedule, and SPC charts, again
with web screens. Slice 3 adds nonconformances (raise, approve, refuse, the NCR every
rejecting usage decision raises in its own transaction) and the 8D correction with its
BR-33.9 due dates, ordered steps, effectiveness check and the `nonconformance.created` and
`capa.closed` events, with list, raise and detail screens. Slice 4 adds incoming vendor
certificates (the requirement on the association and order, receipts checked against the
test group, the VENDOR NCR of an out-of-specification receipt, waivers and the QM-0125
gate on accepting decisions) and physical samples (primary, pooled and retention samples
with their retention expiry and DUE_FOR_DISPOSAL status), with screens on the order and a
sample list. Everything below is a ticket deliverable, acceptance criterion or PRD
rule that is not built yet, or is built only in part. Status is `later slice of PSG-33` (this service, a later slice) or
`handoff` (needs another owner or a path outside this wave).
