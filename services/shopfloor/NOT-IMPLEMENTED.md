# NOT-IMPLEMENTED — PSG-25 (Wave 25) slices 1 to 23

Every PSG-25 deliverable and acceptance criterion that slices 1 to 22 do not build, with its
status and reason. Slice 1 covers the time and attendance core of the shopfloor service
(workers, registrations, pay calculation, approval, review lists, OEE arithmetic), the
supervisor web pages in `apps/web/src/modules/shopfloor/`, and the iot-edge domain core.
Slice 2 adds machines, the HTTP machine-event ingest with BR-25.4 sequence rules, downtimes
with reason codes, andon alerts, the OEE series endpoint, payroll export batches, and their
web pages (machines, downtimes, andon board, OEE, payroll export).
Slice 3 adds the BR-25.1 bundle split and the BR-25.7 device clock correction to the
registration batch, bundle read-back (`GET registrations?bundleId=`), the offline replay
domain rules, the iot-edge policy rules, and the bundle web page.
Slice 4 adds process messages (FR-25.17, BR-25.13): record with category routing and limit
flags, idempotent by source message id, per-destination deliveries with the 1, 5, 15, 60
minute schedule, delivery reports from the consuming contexts, reprocess with a reason,
the monitor API, the `processmessage.received` and `.delivered` events, and the monitor,
detail and manual record web pages.
Slice 5 adds the process message retry sweep (`process-messages:retry-due`, leased on the
1, 5, 15, 60 minute schedule, `skip locked`, tenant-scoped), deletion with a reason for a
message with a destination in error (audit columns, `processmessage.deleted` event), keyset
paging of the monitor (`cursor`, `nextCursor`), and on the web *Load more* on the monitor
and a delete form on the message detail.
Slice 6 adds BR-25.6 idle detection with a per-machine idle threshold, the OEE rollup that
stores buckets (`oee:rollup`, `oee_bucket`, `oeebucket.updated` and `.removed` events), kiosk
badge login (`sessions:badge`), the "Who is clocked in" inquiry (`clocked-in`), the grant of
the process message tables to the application role (missing since migration 0004), and on
the web a kiosk page, the clocked-in board and the idle threshold on machines.
Slice 7 adds the job dispatch projection written by the w23 schedule (`POST jobs`), the
dispatch list and job detail (FR-25.3), operator feedback with good and scrap quantities,
scrap reason and finish, idempotent by client `feedbackId` (FR-25.4), quality checks
(numeric with limits, pass-fail, text) with the SF-0005 finish gate and the supervisor
override with reason (FR-25.5, AC-25.9), the `job.reported` event, and on the web the
dispatch list and the job detail with the report and override forms.
Slice 8 makes registrations drive dispatch (FR-25.3, BR-25.7): a `job-start` moves a released
job to started, an open start auto-stops the worker's other open job or bundle with
server-written closed job-stops (hours, bundle split by BR-25.1, flag `auto-stopped`, warning
SF-0002, capped at 24 hours), a start for a finished job is stored with flag
`job-finished-conflict` (warning SF-0004), the badge session lists the worker's open jobs,
and on the web the kiosk starts jobs and bundles and stops them.
Slice 9 adds supervisor correction of a registration with a reason and an audit trail
(`registrations/{id}:correct`, `registration-corrections`), recalculation of a range with
reversal lines for approved days (`pay:revise`, AC-25.8), the `registration.corrected` and
`payday.revised` events, the AsyncAPI descriptions the doc gates asked for, and on the web the
corrections list, the correction form and the revise pay form.
Slice 10 adds the auto clock-out job (D2h): `registrations:auto-clock-out` closes a clock-in left
open longer than a threshold (4 to 24 hours, default 16) with a flagged clock-out, the review
list `auto-clock-outs`, the duty `shopfloor.time.autoclockout`, SF-0130, the BR-25.3 hold (the
closed day counts the hours but keeps `missing-clock-out` until a supervisor confirms the

> **Security gap (slice 14): connector identity is bound by hand, not by certificate.** The configuration
> pull (which carries the write allowlist) and the heartbeat are refused unless the caller holds
> `shopfloor.edge.connect` and its authenticated subject equals the node's `connectorSubject`, so one
> connector cannot read or report for another node. The binding is set by an edge administrator on the
> node, and it is only as strong as the gateway's authentication of the connector's credential: token
> enrollment and the mTLS client certificate (FR-25.19, BR-25.14) are not built, so nothing ties the
> subject to the certificate serial the connector reports, and the reported serial and expiry are stored
> as reported. Enrollment must set the binding from the issued certificate and the gateway must forward
> the certificate subject; until then the connector credential must be a per-node client (never a shared
> one), issued by the platform identity service.

## Requirements questions

| Item | Question |
|---|---|
| Auto clock-out time | BR-25.3 closes a missing clock-out at shift end once shift end plus 4 h has passed. Shift windows are persisted (slice 11) but the sweep does not read them yet, so this build closes a clock-in open for more than 16 hours (4 to 24 per call) at clock-in plus that threshold, so a shift is never paid for the time the sweep was late, and holds the day for supervisor review. Confirm the 16-hour default until shifts exist, and that the clock-out moves to shift end once profiles are persisted. |
| BR-25.5 / AC-25.4 shift example | The PRD says OEE = 0.7522, but A × P × Q = 0.875 × 0.904762 × 0.95 = 0.752083, which rounds to 0.7521 (the rounded factors give 0.7521 as well). The code follows the formula; the requirements owner should correct the example. |
| AC-25.6 "coding the reason closes the downtime" | Coding sets the reason and category and leaves the downtime open until the machine reports another state, so the downtime keeps measuring the real stop; the alert stays open until it clears, as the AC asks. Confirm that coding should not end the downtime record. |
| BR-25.4 "seq ≤ last seen → dropped" | This build reads "last seen" as "already stored": an event at or below the machine's last stored seq whose `(machine, seq)` is not stored (a replay of a reported gap, or a batch overtaken across a broker restart) is stored and counted as accepted, and only a stored `(machine, seq)` is dropped as a duplicate. Read literally, BR-25.4 drops these events and the documented gap replay could never fill a gap. Such a late event feeds counts and OEE but not the downtime record or andon, which follow the newest state (`TestFloor_LateStateEventsFeedOEENotDowntime`), so a late DOWN has no downtime record to code a reason on. Confirm both. |
| Kiosk stop after a server auto-stop | When a replayed start was closed by the server (older than the worker's newer unit) and its own job-stop arrives in a later request, the server's stop is corrected to zero length with a `registration_correction` row (corrected by `system:offline-replay`) and `registration.corrected`, so the job's hours are the kiosk stop's. Confirm that the kiosk's stop wins over the server's, and that consumers (w21 job card) apply `registration.corrected` to job-stop hours. |
| Andon timing | The 10-minute andon threshold is checked against server receive time when the machine's events arrive; an edge that stops sending entirely gets its andon from `andon:sweep`, which still needs a scheduler to call it (handoff). The reverse also holds: a buffered DOWN delivered more than 10 minutes late raises an andon even when the stop lasted seconds and its end is in a later batch (seen while building `TestHTTP_IoT10kEventsBrokerRestart`, which therefore feeds a live minute). Confirm whether backfilled downtimes should be judged by device time. |
| BR-25.6 idle span | "No count event for idle_threshold_s while state = RUNNING flips the bucket to IDLE from the last count". This build reads it as: when the gap between counts is longer than the threshold, the whole gap from the last count is IDLE (not only the part after the threshold), and RUNNING without any count before a stop is idle when longer than the threshold. Confirm. |
| Idle gap at the end of the data | A RUNNING machine whose last count is older than the threshold and that has sent nothing since is idle up to now, and time after now is not scheduled. An edge that stops publishing (network loss) therefore shows idle, not run time, until its buffered events arrive and the window is rolled up again. Confirm this is wanted over "unknown". |
| Pay week start | BR-25.8 says the pay week start is profile-defined. This build keeps it on the pay agreement (`weekStart`), because it belongs to the overtime rule; a worker without an agreement uses Monday. Confirm, or should it live on the profile? |
| Quantities on registrations and on feedback | The PRD carries good and scrap quantities both on job registrations (`qty_good`, `qty_scrap`) and on `jobs/{id}:feedback`. This build keeps the job's reported totals from feedback only; registration quantities split bundle hours (BR-25.1). Confirm which one the w21 job-card journal reads, or whether the kiosk sends quantities only through feedback. |
| SF-0005 for a check never recorded | FR-25.5 says "a failed mandatory check blocks `finished`". This build also blocks a mandatory check that has no value yet, since an unrecorded check has not passed. Confirm. |
| Bundle members across batches | BR-25.1 says bundle hours are split, not when the jobs arrive. This build requires every closed job of a bundle in one batch (SF-0113 otherwise), because a split cannot be revised once `registration.posted` is published. Confirm the kiosk always closes a bundle in one action, or define a re-split with correction lines (D2g). |
| Which times the skew correction moves | BR-25.7 says skew is "corrected by the offset measured at reconnect". This build applies it to every item of a batch that carries `deviceSentAt`, offline or not, since they share the device clock. Confirm. |
| Delete when a destination is in error | FR-25.17 says failed destinations "stay in error for manual reprocess or deletion (with reason)". Every category routes to Kafka, which is delivered at commit, so the message status is never `error`; this build allows deletion when any destination is in error and leaves delivered destinations as they are. Confirm that deleting is per message, not per destination. |
| Auto-stop of a start replayed late | BR-25.7 orders replays by client time within a batch. When an open start arrives that is older than the worker's open unit (another device, a late replay), this build closes the incoming start at the open unit's start, so the newer work stays open. Confirm, or should the newer unit be split? |
| Auto-stop bundle basis | An auto-stopped bundle is split by the good quantity on its start registrations (usually zero, so equally), as BR-25.1 reads; quantities reported through `jobs/{id}:feedback` are not used. Confirm, together with the "Quantities on registrations and on feedback" question. |
| Auto-stop after more than 24 hours | A registration is at most 24 hours, so a start left open longer is auto-stopped at its start plus 24 hours and flagged `auto-stop-capped` for review, rather than at the new start. Confirm, or should the auto clock-out job (D2h) end it at the shift end? |
| Point job-stops | The PRD has `started_at` and `stopped_at` on every registration. This build stops work with a closed `job-stop` that repeats the job's start; a `job-stop` without `stoppedAt` ends the open start without hours. Confirm the kiosk never needs the server to find the start. |
| Reports after deletion | A consumer that reports on a deleted message gets SF-0120 and nothing is recorded. Confirm the consumers should treat that as final rather than have the outcome stored for audit. |
