## GL posting accounts (placeholder)

Without an `eam_posting_profile` row (`PUT /v1/eam/posting-profiles`, per legal entity and work order type, `ALL` is the wildcard) the service books on placeholder accounts that the accountant must replace: parts expense 6500, labor expense 6510, parts offset 1400, labor offset 6590. The ledger refuses an account that is not in its chart, and a booking on placeholders is flagged `placeholderProfile`. The offset of parts is the inventory or clearing account (part issue is wave 16), of labor the labor absorption account. Set `LEDGER_URL` and the signing key to enable the routes (503 `EAM-GL-003` otherwise).
