build, with its status and reason. Status `later slice of PSG-46` means the work
is owned by this wave and planned after slice 2. `handoff` means it needs a
directory or owner outside this wave's owned paths (`data/`,
`services/analytics/`, `apps/web/src/modules/analytics/`).
