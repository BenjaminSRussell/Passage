# NOT-IMPLEMENTED: analytics (PSG-46, wave 46, slices 1-4)

Every ticket deliverable and acceptance criterion that slices 1 to 4 do not
build, with its status and reason. Status `later slice of PSG-46` means the work
is owned by this wave and planned after slice 4. Slice 4 adds dashboard
definitions, explorer export jobs and the export log, the schedule dispatcher
with delivery attempts and `report.delivered`, and catalog lineage edges with
the lineage walk, each with web screens. Slice 3 adds only the
§9.46.14 inquiry filters (scorecard by asset, rule type and owner; archive
partitions by domain, period and status): its swarm draft duplicated the
slice-2 store and API, which main already had in reviewed form. `handoff` means it needs a
directory or owner outside this wave's owned paths (`data/`,
`services/analytics/`, `apps/web/src/modules/analytics/`).
