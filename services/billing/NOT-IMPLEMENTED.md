# NOT-IMPLEMENTED — billing (PSG-29, wave 29, slices 1–6)

Slice 1 builds free-text invoice proposal, posting, read and list, the totals and
tax-recheck rules, the schema and event contract, and the billing web screens
(`apps/web/src/modules/billing`). Slice 2 changed no billing service behaviour: it
built revenue contracts, milestones, recognition runs and the roll-forward in
`services/revrec`, and the billing web module gained the revenue contract and
roll-forward screens that call revrec. Slice 3 builds credit notes against posted
invoices (whole or by line quantity, BIL-0005 remaining-amount check at proposal and
again under the original's lock at post, gap-free `CN-` numbers, `creditnote.posted`)
and installment plans on free-text proposals (BR-29.11 split, stored, shown and
carried on `invoice.posted`), with the credit-note and installment screens.
Slice 4 adds cursor pagination to the invoices list (`nextCursor` / `cursor`, keyset
on creation time and id) with "Load more" on the invoices and credit-notes screens;
its revenue work (ratable basis, per-contract roll-forward) is in `services/revrec`.
Slice 5 adds invoices from posted packing slips (`POST /v1/billing/proposals`): one
SHIPMENT invoice per order, or one CONSOLIDATED invoice for the request (FR-29.1,
FR-29.2), auto split at slip boundaries above `BILLING_MAX_LINES` (BR-29.5, BIL-0007
for a slip alone over the limit), the BIL-0006 date rule, per-slip charges, the
source references on every line (slip, slip line, slip date, order, order line,
product, price explanation), the rule that a slip line is invoiced once (409
BIL-0104, unique index), the slip and order references on `invoice.posted`, and the
invoice audit trace (`GET /v1/billing/invoices/{id}/trace`, FR-29.12), with the
"Invoice shipments" and trace screens and a type filter on the invoices list.
Slice 6 adds the subscription aggregate (plan price per billing period, start, plan
change, cancel) that issues prorated SUBSCRIPTION invoice proposals through the same
invoice path, and drives the 25 proration edge cases through it. Slice 7 books the invoice journal (FR-29.3, deferred share on 2400) of posted invoices and
credit notes through the ledger client (items 3, 5, 15). Everything below is
outstanding. Status is `later slice of PSG-29` (same service, later build) or
`handoff` (needs another owner, outside this wave's paths).
