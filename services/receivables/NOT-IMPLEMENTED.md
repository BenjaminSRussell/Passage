## Payment lifecycle not in this slice

- `:on-account` and `:reverse` endpoints (BR-30.6): the apply path handles residual on account, but there is no explicit route, no reversal and no re-apply of ON_ACCOUNT payments.
- `SUGGESTED` is never persisted. `:match` returns the decision without storing it, and nothing auto-applies on its own: the clerk applies the AUTO_APPLY candidate from the payment page. Auto-application on import (a job that matches each imported payment and applies AUTO_APPLY) is not built; `store.TestCashApp500_ImportMatchAndAutoApply` drives `MatchPayment` and `ApplyPayment` the way it would.
- Decision rule, as built for AC-30.1: a candidate set made only of the top set's items (a quoted invoice of a multi-invoice payment, scored alone) is not counted as a rival for BR-30.1's "exactly one set ≥ 85" or as the runner-up. Read literally, the rule demotes every multi-invoice payment of a learned payer (each quoted invoice alone reaches R1 + R4 + R5 = 85) to SUGGESTED. A set with any item outside the top set still blocks. This reading of BR-30.1 needs product confirmation.
- `REVERSED` status and the reversal of an auto-application within the open period (BR-30.1) are not built.
- `POST /payments` accepts only source MANUAL. LOCKBOX and CAMT053 payments come from their importers; PORTAL and REMITTANCE have no source yet.
- Settlements are not partitioned by month (budget §5.30.19). A plain table is used.
- Matcher rules R4, R5 and R7 are built in slice 5, with these limits: R4 compares the payer account hash with hashes learned when earlier payments were applied, not with the customer master's bank accounts (wave 7 owns those; it would need a lookup or a `customer.bank-account` event); R5 compares with payer names learned the same way, not with the customer or invoice-account name (customer master); lockbox payments carry no account hash (the BAI2 type 16 record has no MICR; the bank lockbox transmission format is not parsed, deliverable 1); R7 needs `shortPayReason`, which only manual payments carry (remittance files with a short-pay reason are not parsed). Refinement pending product confirmation (BR-30.1): a set whose amount the payment does not explain (no R2, R2-SUBSET, R3 or R7) cannot auto-apply, whatever its R1, R4 and R5 score, because a known payer who quotes the wrong invoice would settle the quoted one; it is returned as SUGGESTED. This also stops reference-only partial payments and overpayments from known payers auto-applying. The BR text allows a reference plus payer identity to reach 85 and auto-apply; this narrows it, and it also keeps short payments without a reason (1-5%) out of auto-apply. Bindings are never unlearned: a mis-application teaches its binding until a reversal path exists. The hash is an unsalted SHA-256 of the normalized account; a keyed hash (HMAC with a Vault key) is a platform handoff.

## Opening-item import (slice 6, integration handoff H5)

Built: `POST /v1/receivables/open-items:batch` (service duty `receivables.import.write`, held by the `migration-loader` principal once the handoff rows below land) loads a cutover snapshot of open AR items for one legal entity from one legacy system (`importSource`, for example `SAP-PRD`). Up to 1000 rows per request, each with legacy key, document number, customer, kind (`INVOICE`, `CREDIT_NOTE`, `PAYMENT` for an unapplied payment, `ADJUSTMENT`), side (`DEBIT` or `CREDIT`), document and due dates, currency, original and open amount (magnitudes; the side gives the sign) and optional cash discount. Credit-side items are stored negative (migration 0011 makes the amount and open-amount constraints sign-aware). Idempotent and re-runnable per (source, legacy key): a new key creates, an untouched known item is updated to the snapshot (a legacy partial payment is just a lower open amount), an identical row writes nothing, and an item that was settled, partly settled, paid, cleared against a down payment or deducted in Passage (ever, even if that was reversed) is refused with `AR-OPENING-SETTLED` and left as it is; one with an active dispute or a promise to pay that still holds is refused with `AR-OPENING-IN-COLLECTION`. Other per-row refusals: `AR-OPENING-INVALID` (field rules), `AR-OPENING-DUPLICATE-KEY` (twice in a batch), `AR-OPENING-IDENTITY` (customer, currency, legal entity, kind or side of a loaded key changed, including a key already loaded into a legal entity outside the caller's scope), `AR-OPENING-DOCUMENT-EXISTS` (document number taken by another item in the legal entity). Results are per row; `atomic: true` stores nothing unless every row is accepted (422 with every row reported). One `openitem.imported.v1` per batch that created or updated an item. `GET /open-item-imports` totals the imported items per source, legal entity and currency (debit, credit, net) for the reconciliation, and `GET /open-items?importSource=` filters the list. Credit items are kept out of the match pool, dunning, interest and the payment apply, dispute, deduction and down payment paths. Since the slice-6 review credits also net in the worklist (which still lists only customers with a debit to work), the risk score and the balance snapshot; migration 0012 drops the snapshot's `total_open >= 0` check, so `customerbalance.changed.v1` can carry a negative balance.

Open points for the loader and later waves (found in review, not built):

- The document number must be unique per legal entity (`open_item` unique (tenant, legal entity, document number)). SAP repeats a document number across fiscal years and an AX or SAP document can have several open lines, so the loader must compose `documentNumber` (for example `<BELNR>-<GJAHR>-<BUZEI>`) and keep the raw number in `legacyKey`; a plain number clash is refused with `AR-OPENING-DOCUMENT-EXISTS`.
- No accounting-currency amount is stored for an imported item (`amount_entity_currency`, §5.30.4). Wave 49's reconciliation (BR-49.2) compares open items per control account with the migrated GL balance in accounting currency and expects it; the batch takes only the document-currency amounts.
- The import posts nothing to the ledger. Per BR-49.2 the open items load against an opening-balance clearing account and the GL opening balance is migrated by the ledger pack; receivables writes no journal and reconciles only through `GET /open-item-imports`.
- The import totals do not split credit notes (control account 1200) from unapplied payments (1205): `creditOpen` sums every credit-side item, so the BR-49.2 check per control account needs a per-kind split (`CREDIT_NOTE` and credit `ADJUSTMENT` against 1200, `PAYMENT` against 1205) that is not built.

Not built: an `Idempotency-Key` header on the batch (the legacy key is the natural key and a replay of the same body changes nothing); an import batch table or a stored per-row result history (the event and the totals are the record); `amount_entity_currency` and `special_gl_type` for imported items; locking a legal entity's imported items after cutover sign-off; reading customers from party to check that `customerId` exists (the loader resolves it through its xref); posting the imported balance to the ledger (the ledger opening balance is the ledger wave's own migration; reconcile with the totals read); settling credit items against debits.

## Ledger posting

- RMA credit notes post Dr 4000 / Cr 4200 / Cr 1200 through `ledgerclient` when `LEDGER_URL` is set, to the ledger `RECEIVABLES_RMA_LEDGERS` pins for the legal entity (the request names no ledger). Settlement and payment postings (Dr 1000/1010, Cr 1200; Dr 4100 for cash discount; Dr 6100 for write-off; FX 7100/7200) are not posted through `ledger.Post`. `journal_posting_id` is set only on RMA credits.
- Down payment and deposit receipts, clearings and releases carry their lines on the `downpayment.*` events only.
- Deduction write-offs (Dr 6110 / Cr 1200) carry their lines on `deduction.resolved.v1` only. Moving a deduction off its item and re-billing a denial stay inside 1200 and post nothing.
- Dunning fees (Cr 4300) and interest (Cr 4310) are open items only; their journal postings wait for the same ledger client. Reconciliation of fees and interest to GL is therefore not yet checked.
- The `ledgerclient` package is wired for RMA credits (`LEDGER_URL`). Other receivables journals still wait on the same client. Reconciliation Σ open_amount = GL 1200 is therefore not yet checked.

## Platform and kernel gaps (handoffs)

- **Readiness.** `httpx.NewRouter` serves a static `/readyz`. The service's `API.Ready` pings Postgres but is not mounted, because chi rejects a second route. Handoff: the kernel should accept a readiness hook.
- **Idempotency.** Create uses the `idempotency_key` column on `payment` (unique per tenant). `:apply` stores the key and response in `idempotency_keys` and replays them. The key is optional on apply (If-Match already guards it); a kernel middleware would make it uniform across services.
- **Rate-limit, `Deprecation` and `X-Passage-Api-Build` headers** are kernel middleware, not added here.
- **Authentication.** Identity comes from the `tenant` middleware headers until the wave 2 JWT and OPA path is wired at the gateway. Handoff to edge-identity.
