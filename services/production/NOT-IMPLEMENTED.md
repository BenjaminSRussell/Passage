## GL posting (shared `platform/go/ledgerclient`)

Set `LEDGER_URL` (chart `ledgerUrl`) and the service signing key (`serviceIdentity.signingKeySecret`); the service signs only `ledger.entry.post` and `ledger.balance.read`. Without `LEDGER_URL` both GL routes answer 503 `PRODUCTION_GL_UNAVAILABLE`. One entry per posted journal and one per settlement, keyed by journal or order id; a refused group (a closed period) is reported under `failed` and retried by the next run. The reconciliation is per WIP account and currency, GL minus subledger. No placeholder accounts: the existing posting profile supplies every account.
