# NOT-IMPLEMENTED: intercompany (PSG-40, wave 40)

Slices 1 to 3 are stateless: pure domain rules, the journal, charge,
centralized payment, payment reversal, delivery chain and planning previews,
the reconciliation classify endpoint, the transfer-pricing evidence export,
and the web pages for each. Slice 4 stores trading-partner pairings (with
the outbox and idempotency keys), prices IC lines and checks linked order
changes against a stored pairing, and adds the partner screens. Every
deliverable and acceptance criterion not listed as built in the README is
below, with its status and reason.
