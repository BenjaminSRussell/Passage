The level rules are checked at the If-Match version only, so a retry with the same Idempotency-Key after the requisition moved to a level with another role returns the original answer instead of that level's refusal; PRC-REQ-010 counts approvals since the last submit by requisition version, so an approver of a rejected round may approve the resubmitted one. `:reject` (same duty, required comment, not held to the limit) moves IN_REVIEW to REJECTED with `requisition.rejected.v1` (level, role, reason). The requester (procurement.requisition.create) can `:reopen` a REJECTED requisition to DRAFT (chain cleared, so submit routes it afresh) and `:cancel` (the web page asks to confirm) a DRAFT, REJECTED or APPROVED one: an APPROVED requisition only while every line is OPEN (a line already ordered stays with its PO) and no running RFQ holds a line (PRC-REQ-006); its OPEN lines become CANCELLED, they leave the consolidation queue, and `requisition.cancelled.v1` lists them. `GET /requisition-approvals` (procurement.requisition.approve or procurement.purchase-order.inquire; filters `role`, `legalEntityId`) is the approver's inbox, newest first, without the caller's own requisitions. `GET /requisitions/{id}` is also open to the approve duty and returns `approvedSteps`, `nextApprover` and `history`. Migration 0030 adds `purchase_requisition.approved_steps` and `purchase_requisition_history` (submitted, each decision, reopened, cancelled: actor, level, role, comment, statuses, version; RLS tenant and legal entity, SELECT and INSERT for passage_app). The web module gains the Requisition approvals inbox (nav item gated on `procurement.purchase-order.inquire`), and the requisition page gains the approval progress, the decision form (approve, send back with a reason), reopen, cancel and the history table.
