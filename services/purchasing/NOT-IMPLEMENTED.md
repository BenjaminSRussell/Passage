# NOT-IMPLEMENTED — purchasing (PSG-17, wave 17)

Slice 1 builds the core of the document chain: requisitions with band/category
approval routing, purchase orders priced from trade agreements with a database
total identity, trade agreement price rows with the BR-17.3 overlap check, price
lookup with explain, lists for all three documents, the migration with RLS, the
HTTP surface, contracts, chart, Dockerfile and the procurement workspace web
module (`apps/web/src/modules/purchasing`). Slice 2 adds consolidation of approved
requisition lines into POs with the consolidation queue (FR-17.3, AC-17.9),
purchase agreements with release tracking, HARD/SOFT/NONE limits, validity,
`agreement.threshold.v1` and price override (FR-17.5, BR-17.10, AC-17.7 at PO
creation), agreement activate/hold/close, and the agreement and consolidation
screens. Slice 3 adds the PO approval lifecycle (`:submit`, `:approve`, `:reject`,
`:confirm`, `:cancel`, BR-17.2), PATCH with If-Match/412 (BR-17.8), versioned
change management with snapshots, RFC 6902 diffs and the default reapproval rules
(FR-17.8, AC-17.2/17.3), the received/invoiced floor (PRC-PO-007, BR-17.7), line
cancellation that returns agreement releases and requisition lines (BR-17.9),
`po.confirmed`, `poversion.created` and `po.cancelled`, and the PO lifecycle,
edit and version screens. Slice 4 adds the RFQ cycle (FR-17.4, BR-17.11, AC-17.8):
RFQs with lines and a reply deadline, `:send` to invited vendors with
`rfq.sent.v1`, reply capture with late flagging, the per-line compare ranking,
`:close`/`:cancel`, and `:award` creating one DRAFT PO per winning vendor and
currency with `rfq.awarded.v1`, plus the RFQ list, create and detail (send, reply,
compare and award) screens and the "RFQs awaiting replies" workspace tile.
Slice 5 adds sealed-bid RFQs and weighted scoring (FR-17.19, BR-17.16, AC-17.14):
the `sealed` flag with replies hidden until the deadline, 403 PRC-RFQ-004 on
compare, scoring and award before it, late replies refused (PRC-RFQ-007), the
opening log (`openedAt`/`openedBy`), scoring criteria with weights, buyer scores
1–10 per invited vendor and the WEIGHTED_SCORE ranking; and invoicing plans on PO
lines (FR-17.21, BR-17.17, AC-17.15): PERIODIC and PARTIAL plans generated from
the line amount, reproportioned inside the PO edit when the line amount changes
and removed with a cancelled line, with the sealed-bid and scoring parts of the
RFQ screens and an invoicing plan page per PO line. Slice 6 adds the subsequent
price change (FR-17.22, BR-17.18, AC-17.16): `POST /purchase-orders/{id}:reprice`
and the run `POST /purchase-orders:reprice` (preview with PurchaseOrderInquire,
apply with PurchaseOrderConfirm), moving open trade-agreement-priced lines to the
row that wins on the order date as the next PO version through the change
management path, handing invoiced lines to payables once per price row,
`po.repriced.v1`, and the price change pages (one PO, and the run); plus the
BR-17.13 scheduling agreement zone rules as pure domain functions. Slice 7
documented the row-level-security test role. Slice 8 adds scheduling agreements
and releases (FR-17.15, BR-17.13, AC-17.11): agreements per vendor × product ×
site with a unit price, target quantity, validity and release profile; the
release schedule with zones, reason codes on firm-zone reductions, trade-off
warnings and the receipt floor; FRC/JIT release runs with cumulative released and
received quantities, the cumulative reset, `sarelease.created.v1`; the release
monitor and release documentation; and the scheduling agreement screens.
Slice 9 brings the OpenAPI and AsyncAPI specs to zero FAIL lines on the doc gates (`x-passage-duty` on every operation, `x-passage-type` on every message) and adds the purchasing policy per legal entity (FR-17.13): change management default, PO four-eyes segregation, and invoice-before-receipt (BR-17.7), with `policy.changed.v1` and the policy screens.
Slice 10 adds the procurement catalog (FR-17.18, AC-17.13 in part): catalogs per
legal entity with vendor entries (price, unit, currency, lead time, preferred
flag, image reference), published to operating units for a validity window
(DRAFT → PUBLISHED → RETIRED, `catalog.published.v1` and `catalog.retired.v1`),
entry add and remove while DRAFT, the requisition picker search
(`GET /catalog-entries`, by legal entity, unit, text, category and vendor), the
requisition line `catalogEntryId` that fills the line from the entry and refuses an
unavailable catalog (PRC-CAT-004), another currency (PRC-CAT-006) or another unit or
product, and the catalog list, create form, detail page, the picker on the
requisition form (with item images and prices) and the Catalog or Non-catalog mark
on requisition lines. PRC-CAT-001, -004, -006 and -007 are service codes the PRD
message catalog (3.17.18) does not list; it names only -002, -003 and -005.
Slice 11 adds the vendor catalog import (FR-17.18, AC-17.13 import half):
`POST /catalogs/{id}/vendor-import` stages a vendor CSV (or JSON rows) of up to
50,000 rows against a DRAFT or PUBLISHED catalog, each row ACCEPTABLE or ERROR with
its reason (PRC-CAT-005: a missing unit, bad price or currency, a part number
repeated in the file or already held by the vendor in the catalog); the buyer
accepts or rejects rows (`POST /catalog-imports/{id}/decisions`, an ERROR row cannot
be accepted) and `:commit` adds the accepted rows to the catalog as entries with the
catalog version bump and `catalogimport.committed.v1` in one transaction, `:cancel`
drops a staged file; lists and reads of imports and rows; the same file (vendor
and rows) staged once per catalog until its import is cancelled (PRD 3.17.16 keys
the import on the file hash); a commit stopped by a part number another import
committed after staging; the catalog entry cap raised from 2,000 to 50,000 so a
full vendor file fits (the PRD caps the file at 50,000 rows and names no catalog
cap), with catalog reads carrying the count and the first 200 entries and
`GET /catalogs/{id}/entries` paging the rest; and the vendor imports list, the
file or paste upload form, the row review page with accept and reject, accept
all, commit and cancel, and paged entries on the catalog page. The commit into a
PUBLISHED catalog is deliberate: AC-17.13 starts from a published catalog and has
the accepted rows appear in the picker, so the slice 10 rule (a published catalog
is frozen) now reads "no entry of a published catalog is changed or removed, and
only a reviewed vendor import adds to it". PRC-CAT-005, -008, -009 and the
PRC-CAT-IMPORT-* codes are documented in the README.
Slice 12 adds category vendor assignment, category managers and the category access
rule (FR-17.20, PRC-CAT-002, AC-17.12 category half): `procurement_category_vendor`
(APPROVED, PROVISIONAL or BLOCKED with a validity window, one row per vendor and
category, If-Match changes) and `procurement_category_manager`, with
`categoryvendor.assigned.v1`, `categoryvendor.changed.v1`,
`categorymanager.assigned.v1` and `categorymanager.removed.v1`; the policy setting
`categoryAccessMode` (HARD, SOFT, OFF; default SOFT) per legal entity; the rule
applied at requisition submit, at consolidation (including a buyer's vendor
override), at PO creation for every line with a category (a PO line now carries
`category`, copied from the requisition line it orders or given on a direct line) and
at a PO edit for the lines it adds, refusing with 422 PRC-CAT-002 under HARD and
returning warnings under SOFT (PRC-CAT-002) and for PROVISIONAL vendors (PRC-CAT-010); and the category vendor list, create and detail
pages, the category managers page, the access mode on the policy page and the
warnings on the requisition and consolidation screens.
Slice 13 adds signing limits (FR-17.17, BR-17.15, PRC-SL-001, AC-17.12 limit half):
`signing_limit` per worker × document type × currency of a legal entity (spending
limit, approval limit, recertification date) and `signing_limit_request`
(PENDING, APPROVED, REJECTED); a limit is written only by approving a request
(`:approve`, `:reject`, If-Match, the requester and the worker cannot decide, PRC-SL-003)
and recertified with `:recertify` for 12 months; `signinglimit.changed.v1`; the
requester rule at requisition submit (a spending limit below the total, or an expired
limit with warning PRC-SL-001, appends the `NEXT_APPROVER` level) and the approver
rule at PO `:approve` (an approval limit below the PO total, or no limit in the PO
currency, is 403 PRC-SL-002, an expired limit 403 PRC-SL-001); the limit evaluation inquiry; and the signing limit
list, detail with recertify, request list, create and detail screens with approve and
reject. Everything below is absent or partial.

Status values: **later slice of PSG-17** (this wave, next slices) or **handoff**
