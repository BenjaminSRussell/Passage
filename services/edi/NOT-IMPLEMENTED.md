# NOT-IMPLEMENTED: edi (PSG-35, wave 35)

Slice 1 covers the X12 codec (00401 and 00501), 997 acknowledgments, the 850 mapping, partner profiles (create, list, read), inbound X12 receipt with duplicate detection, a read-only interchange list and detail, the map test harness and the web screens for them. Slice 2 adds the outbound 856 (S/O/P/I with SSCC-18), 810 and 855 builders, outbound generation through `POST /edi/v1/partners/{id}/outbound` on the gap-free control sequence, matching of the partner's 997 to the outbound interchange with the `dev.passage.edi.message.acked.v1` event, a direction filter on the interchange list, and the "Send EDI document" screen. Slice 3 adds the message monitor (`GET /edi/v1/messages`, `/messages/{id}`), inbound reprocess from the raw payload with a dry run (`POST /edi/v1/interchanges/{id}:reprocess`), the `dev.passage.edi.message.failed.v1` event, the partner SLA dashboard (`GET /edi/v1/partners/sla`, with 997 latency from `interchange.acked_at`) and their web screens; in `integration-hub-ts`, the quota token bucket and subscription event-type patterns. Slice 4 adds the raw payload viewer as a segment tree with X12 dictionary names (`GET /edi/v1/interchanges/{id}/payload`), BR-35.12 masking of party names, addresses and contacts without `edi.pii.view` (in the tree and in canonical documents), bulk reprocess of up to 200 interchanges with a dry run (`POST /edi/v1/interchanges:reprocess`), the first X12 fixtures under `testdata/x12/`, and their web screens. Slice 5 adds pure rules and stateless dry-run routes for four deliverables: the Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 UBL 2.1 builder for invoices and credit notes (`POST /edi/v1/peppol/invoices:build`), the cXML envelope codec with the BR-35.14 payloadID decision and Response classification (domain only), punchout cart validation for cXML and SAP OCI (BR-35.13, `POST /edi/v1/punchout/returns:parse`), the self-billing matcher (BR-35.15, `POST /edi/v1/self-billing:match`) and their web screens; in `integration-hub-ts`, the PLM import precedence (BR-35.16). Nothing slice 5 adds is persisted. Slice 6 (#483) adds the X12 820 and 214 builders and maps (820 and 214 are outbound sets of `/partners/{id}/outbound`), the EDIFACT codec with ORDERS, DESADV and INVOIC mappings, CONTRL build and parse, and the `internal/transport` package (AS2 with CMS signatures and synchronous MDNs, SFTP, loopback). Slice 7 (#483) wires the transports into the service: a transport per partner (`PUT|GET /partners/{id}/transport`, API, AS2 or SFTP, scoped to the trade profile's legal entity, secrets referenced by name from `EDI_SECRETS_DIR` and never stored), AS2 encryption (CMS EnvelopedData, AES-CBC) and SHA-384/512 signatures, outbound delivery by a leasing dispatcher with retries, `sent` status, the `dev.passage.edi.message.sent.v1` event and `:resend`, asynchronous MDNs both ways, the AS2 listener for partners (`EDI_AS2_ADDR`, `/as2/v1/{endpoint}` and `/mdn`) feeding the X12 receipt pipeline with duplicate Message-ID detection, the 997 sent back over the partner's transport, and the SFTP poller (bounded, idempotent by name and SHA-256, archive, back-off and park). The AS2 hardening (#483) adds the AS2 edge (Gateway `passage-as2` on `as2.<env>.passage.dev`, outside the passage-api JWT policy, one route to the listener, TLS, an 8 MiB edge buffer, a rate limit per client address, a NetworkPolicy admitting only that gateway to the AS2 port), a token bucket per AS2 endpoint in the listener, durable asynchronous MDNs we owe (`transport_mdn`, stored with the receipt and sent by the dispatcher), the AWAITING_MDN timeout and `dev.passage.edi.delivery.failed.v1`. Slice 8 (#483) adds inbound EDIFACT: `POST /edi/v1/inbound/edifact` and EDIFACT over the AS2 listener and the SFTP poller (syntax told by the first segment), UNG/UNE functional groups, the partner's UNB identity and CONTRL mode (migration 0008), the UNB 0020 duplicate window, ORDERS/DESADV/INVOIC mapped onto the 850/856/810 canonical documents with `message.received`, CONTRL answers (UCI, UCF, UCM, UCS, UCD) on a gap-free EDIFACT counter queued over the partner's transport, and negative 997s and CONTRLs for payloads an authenticated transport brought and the receipt refused for syntax or sender. Slice 9 (#483 follow-ups) adds outbound EDIFACT (ORDERS, DESADV and INVOIC on the body route, DESADV and INVOIC from the source services, on the partner's gap-free EDIFACT counter, over its transport) with the partner's CONTRL matched to it like a 997 (`acked_at`, `message.acked`). Everything else in PSG-35 is listed here.
