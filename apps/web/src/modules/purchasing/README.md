# Purchasing web module

Procurement workspace for wave 17 (PSG-17), wired to `services/purchasing` through
`/api/v1/procurement` (see `api/client.ts` and the service's `api/openapi.yaml`).

| Route | Page | Service calls |
|---|---|---|
| `/purchasing` | Workspace: tiles for requisitions in review, approved requisitions, lines needing a vendor, RFQs awaiting replies, draft POs, POs in review, POs to confirm, effective purchase agreements, active price rows; links to each area | `GET requisitions`, `requisition-lines`, `rfqs`, `purchase-orders`, `agreements`, `trade-agreements` (filters, `limit=200`; a full page shows `200+`) |
| `/purchasing/requisitions` | List with status and "only mine" filters, load more | `GET /requisitions` |
| `/purchasing/requisitions/new` | Header and line editor; blank estimates default from the preferred vendor's trade agreement; PRC-REQ-003 warnings shown on the detail page | `POST /requisitions` (Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/requisitions/:id` | Summary, approver chain, lines with the PO line that orders each consolidated line, Submit for approval | `GET /requisitions/{id}`, `POST /requisitions/{id}:submit` (If-Match + Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/consolidation` | Consolidation board: open lines of approved requisitions (all / needs sourcing / has a vendor), select lines, override the vendor per line, set the delivery window, create POs and link to them | `GET /requisition-lines`, `POST /requisitions:consolidate` (Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/open-supply` | Open supply: lines of confirmed POs still to receive, with warehouse, site, open quantity, due and vendor-confirmed date; filters by warehouse, site, product and due range, load more. The PO and requisition line forms take the warehouse a line is delivered to, and the PO lines table shows it | `GET /purchase-order-lines` (cursor paging) |
| `/purchasing/inquiries`, `/purchasing/inquiries/overdue`, `/purchasing/inquiries/backordered`, `/purchasing/inquiries/expiring-agreements`, `/purchasing/inquiries/near-limit-agreements`, `/purchasing/inquiries/change-orders`, `/purchasing/inquiries/price-history` | PO inquiries (PRD 3.17.14) with a shared sub-navigation: open orders by vendor, overdue lines, backordered lines (ordered, received and backordered quantity, open value, due date, days late), expiring agreements (ending within a look-ahead of 30 days by default, lapsed ones marked, committed and released value), agreements near limit (lines released to 90 % or the chosen share of their commitment, overruns marked), change orders (confirmed POs with a change waiting for re-approval, confirmed and proposed totals) and order price history; vendor, product, as-of, days-ahead and release-level filters, load more | `GET /inquiries/open-orders-by-vendor`, `/inquiries/overdue-lines`, `/inquiries/backordered-lines`, `/inquiries/expiring-agreements`, `/inquiries/near-limit-agreements`, `/inquiries/change-orders`, `/inquiries/price-history` |
| `/purchasing/rfqs` | RFQ list with status filter, a Sealed badge, deadline and line / vendor / reply counts, load more | `GET /rfqs` |
| `/purchasing/rfqs/new` | Legal entity, reply deadline (local time, sent as an instant), ranking rule (weighted score needs a criterion), sealed bids, scoring criteria with weights, lines with product or description, quantity, unit and required date | `POST /rfqs` (Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/rfqs/:id` | Summary and lines; Send to vendors (draft); invited vendors and replies with Late / Awarded flags; Record a reply per vendor (lines without a price are not quoted; PRC-RFQ-003 late warning); Compare and award: ranking per line by either rule, rank 1 preselected, late replies set apart and awardable only with a reason, links to the draft POs created; Close replies / Cancel RFQ. Sealed RFQs: bidding status (sealed until the deadline, or opened at / by), a sealed-replies count instead of the replies before the deadline and no comparison; scoring criteria; Score vendors (1–10 per criterion, current scores per vendor) once the replies are open; weighted scores in the comparison | `GET /rfqs/{id}`, `POST /rfqs/{id}:send|close|cancel|award` (If-Match + Idempotency-Key), `POST /rfqs/{id}/replies`, `GET /rfqs/{id}/compare`, `PUT /rfqs/{id}/scores/{vendorId}` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders` | List with approval-status filter, load more | `GET /purchase-orders` |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/new` | Header, lines (blank unit price = agreement override or trade agreement; optional agreement line), header charges, each optionally from a charge code (which sets the calculation and the debit and credit sides), and a box that adds the matching auto charges when saving | `POST /purchase-orders`, `GET /charge-codes` |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id` | Summary, totals panel, lines with price source, received quantity, status and a link to each line's invoicing plan (or to add one) and delivery schedule (or to split the line), charges, version history with each change in business terms; Submit / Approve / Reject / Confirm and send / Cancel order (with a confirmation step) as the status allows; Update prices on a confirmed order; PRC-AGR-004 warnings after create | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `GET /purchase-orders/{id}/versions`, `GET /purchase-orders/{id}/invoicing-plans`, `POST /purchase-orders/{id}:submit|approve|reject|confirm|cancel` (If-Match + Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id/edit` | Edit (before confirmation) or change order (after): existing lines keep their price when the unit price is left blank, a line left out is cancelled, new lines are added; says whether the change was confirmed at once or needs re-approval; PRC-PO-009 when someone else changed the order | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `PATCH /purchase-orders/{id}` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id/lines/:lineNo/invoicing-plan` | One line's invoicing plan: the line and its amount, the schedule (rows, due dates, amounts, percents, open / invoiced) or an empty state, a form for a periodic (start, monthly / quarterly, periods, billing day, amount per period) or milestone (dates with percents or amounts) plan with "invoice without receipt", and Remove plan with a confirmation step; no form for a cancelled line or order, or once a row is invoiced | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `GET`, `PUT`, `DELETE /purchase-orders/{id}/lines/{lineNo}/invoicing-plan` (If-Match = PO row version) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id/lines/:lineNo/delivery-schedule` | One line's delivery schedule (FR-17.10): the line, the deliveries (quantity, requested and vendor-confirmed date) or an empty state, a form that splits the line into deliveries with a live "still to schedule" balance while the order is a draft or rejected, Remove schedule with a confirmation step, and a confirmed date per delivery on a confirmed order; no form otherwise | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `GET`, `PUT`, `DELETE /purchase-orders/{id}/lines/{lineNo}/delivery-schedule`, `PUT .../delivery-schedule/rows/{seq}/confirmation` (If-Match = PO row version) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id/reprice` | Price change for one confirmed order (linked as "Update prices" from the order): the read-only preview loads at once with the pricing date, lines that move (old and new price, amount and signed change), invoiced lines sent to payables and lines left alone with the reason; Apply price change under the previewed row version, then whether the new version is confirmed or waits for re-approval; PRC-PO-009 offers Preview again; a warning and no preview for an order that is not confirmed | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `POST /purchase-orders/{id}:reprice` (preview; apply with If-Match + Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/reprice` | Price change run: optional trade agreement row, vendor and legal entity filters, preview one page of confirmed orders with lines to move or hand over (link to each order's price change), apply that page (one key per previewed page), per-order result with the failure code, next page | `POST /purchase-orders:reprice` (apply with Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/agreements` | Purchase agreement list with status filter and highest release %, load more | `GET /agreements` |

Pages use the shell primitives from `src/shell/ui` and `pages.module.css` tokens only.
Every data page has loading, empty and error states; errors show the service's stable
problem code. Amounts stay decimal strings from the service and are never parsed to
floats. Ids are typed as UUIDs until the vendor, product and legal-entity pickers from
waves 6 to 8 are available to modules.
