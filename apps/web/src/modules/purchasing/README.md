# Purchasing web module

Procurement workspace for wave 17 (PSG-17), wired to `services/purchasing` through
`/api/v1/procurement` (see `api/client.ts` and the service's `api/openapi.yaml`).

| Route | Page | Service calls |
|---|---|---|
| `/purchasing` | Workspace: tiles for requisitions in review, approved requisitions, lines needing a vendor, RFQs awaiting replies, draft POs, POs in review, POs to confirm, effective purchase agreements, active price rows; links to each area | `GET requisitions`, `requisition-lines`, `rfqs`, `purchase-orders`, `agreements`, `trade-agreements` (filters, `limit=200`; a full page shows `200+`) |
| `/purchasing/requisitions` | List with status and "only mine" filters, load more | `GET /requisitions` |
| `/purchasing/requisitions/new` | Header and line editor; blank estimates default from the preferred vendor's trade agreement; PRC-REQ-003 warnings shown on the detail page | `POST /requisitions` (Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/requisitions/:id` | Summary, approver chain, lines with the PO line that orders each consolidated line, Submit for approval | `GET /requisitions/{id}`, `POST /requisitions/{id}:submit` (If-Match + Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/consolidation` | Consolidation board: open lines of approved requisitions (all / needs sourcing / has a vendor), select lines, override the vendor per line, set the delivery window, create POs and link to them | `GET /requisition-lines`, `POST /requisitions:consolidate` (Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/rfqs` | RFQ list with status filter, a Sealed badge, deadline and line / vendor / reply counts, load more | `GET /rfqs` |
| `/purchasing/rfqs/new` | Legal entity, reply deadline (local time, sent as an instant), ranking rule (weighted score needs a criterion), sealed bids, scoring criteria with weights, lines with product or description, quantity, unit and required date | `POST /rfqs` (Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/rfqs/:id` | Summary and lines; Send to vendors (draft); invited vendors and replies with Late / Awarded flags; Record a reply per vendor (lines without a price are not quoted; PRC-RFQ-003 late warning); Compare and award: ranking per line by either rule, rank 1 preselected, late replies set apart and awardable only with a reason, links to the draft POs created; Close replies / Cancel RFQ. Sealed RFQs: bidding status (sealed until the deadline, or opened at / by), a sealed-replies count instead of the replies before the deadline and no comparison; scoring criteria; Score vendors (1–10 per criterion, current scores per vendor) once the replies are open; weighted scores in the comparison | `GET /rfqs/{id}`, `POST /rfqs/{id}:send|close|cancel|award` (If-Match + Idempotency-Key), `POST /rfqs/{id}/replies`, `GET /rfqs/{id}/compare`, `PUT /rfqs/{id}/scores/{vendorId}` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders` | List with approval-status filter, load more | `GET /purchase-orders` |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/new` | Header, lines (blank unit price = agreement override or trade agreement; optional agreement line), header charges | `POST /purchase-orders` |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id` | Summary, totals panel, lines with price source, received quantity, status and a link to each line's invoicing plan (or to add one) and delivery schedule (or to split the line), charges, version history with each change in business terms; Submit / Approve / Reject / Confirm and send / Cancel order (with a confirmation step) as the status allows; Update prices on a confirmed order; PRC-AGR-004 warnings after create | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `GET /purchase-orders/{id}/versions`, `GET /purchase-orders/{id}/invoicing-plans`, `POST /purchase-orders/{id}:submit|approve|reject|confirm|cancel` (If-Match + Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id/edit` | Edit (before confirmation) or change order (after): existing lines keep their price when the unit price is left blank, a line left out is cancelled, new lines are added; says whether the change was confirmed at once or needs re-approval; PRC-PO-009 when someone else changed the order | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `PATCH /purchase-orders/{id}` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id/lines/:lineNo/invoicing-plan` | One line's invoicing plan: the line and its amount, the schedule (rows, due dates, amounts, percents, open / invoiced) or an empty state, a form for a periodic (start, monthly / quarterly, periods, billing day, amount per period) or milestone (dates with percents or amounts) plan with "invoice without receipt", and Remove plan with a confirmation step; no form for a cancelled line or order, or once a row is invoiced | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `GET`, `PUT`, `DELETE /purchase-orders/{id}/lines/{lineNo}/invoicing-plan` (If-Match = PO row version) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id/lines/:lineNo/delivery-schedule` | One line's delivery schedule (FR-17.10): the line, the deliveries (quantity, requested and vendor-confirmed date) or an empty state, a form that splits the line into deliveries with a live "still to schedule" balance while the order is a draft or rejected, Remove schedule with a confirmation step, and a confirmed date per delivery on a confirmed order; no form otherwise | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `GET`, `PUT`, `DELETE /purchase-orders/{id}/lines/{lineNo}/delivery-schedule`, `PUT .../delivery-schedule/rows/{seq}/confirmation` (If-Match = PO row version) |
| `/purchasing/purchase-orders/:id/reprice` | Price change for one confirmed order (linked as "Update prices" from the order): the read-only preview loads at once with the pricing date, lines that move (old and new price, amount and signed change), invoiced lines sent to payables and lines left alone with the reason; Apply price change under the previewed row version, then whether the new version is confirmed or waits for re-approval; PRC-PO-009 offers Preview again; a warning and no preview for an order that is not confirmed | `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `POST /purchase-orders/{id}:reprice` (preview; apply with If-Match + Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/reprice` | Price change run: optional trade agreement row, vendor and legal entity filters, preview one page of confirmed orders with lines to move or hand over (link to each order's price change), apply that page (one key per previewed page), per-order result with the failure code, next page | `POST /purchase-orders:reprice` (apply with Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/agreements` | Purchase agreement list with status filter and highest release %, load more | `GET /agreements` |
| `/purchasing/agreements/new` | Header, validity, start as draft or effective; commitment lines whose fields follow the commitment type | `POST /agreements` (Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/agreements/:id` | Summary, lines with committed / released / remaining / %, releases linked to their POs, Activate / Put on hold / Close | `GET /agreements/{id}`, `POST /agreements/{id}:activate|hold|close` (If-Match + Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/scheduling-agreements` | Monitor scheduling agreement releases: vendor, product, validity, open quantity, release count, last release and run date, next release due with the frequency, load more | `GET /scheduling-agreements` |
| `/purchasing/scheduling-agreements/new` | Legal entity, vendor, product and site, currency, unit and unit price, target quantity, validity; release profile (firm and trade-off zones, JIT and FRC horizons, frequency, PRD defaults filled in); the dated deliveries | `POST /scheduling-agreements` (Idempotency-Key) |
| `/purchasing/scheduling-agreements/:id` | Summary with price, zones and horizons; the schedule with each line's zone (Firm / Trade-off / Forecast), scheduled, received and open quantity and last reduction reason; Edit schedule (change date or quantity, add, remove; a firm line needs a reason code to be reduced, moved or removed; trade-off warnings shown after saving; PRC-VERSION-CONFLICT when someone else changed it); Run a release (FRC or JIT, run date, optional cumulative reset; says when an unchanged schedule returned the existing release; PRC-SA-003 shown); the releases linked to their documentation | `GET /scheduling-agreements/{id}`, `PUT /scheduling-agreements/{id}/schedule` (If-Match), `POST /scheduling-agreements/{id}:release` (Idempotency-Key), `GET /scheduling-agreements/{id}/releases` |
| `/purchasing/scheduling-agreements/:id/releases/:releaseNo` | Release documentation: kind, run date, schedule version, cumulative released and received, reset flag; lines in date order with zone, in-horizon flag and cumulative quantities | `GET /scheduling-agreements/{id}/releases/{releaseNo}` |
| `/purchasing/trade-agreements` | Price row journal, load more | `GET /trade-agreements` |
| `/purchasing/trade-agreements/new` | New PRICE row; PRC-TA-002 overlap shown inline | `POST /trade-agreements` |
| `/purchasing/trade-agreements/:id` | One price row | `GET /trade-agreements/{id}` |
| `/purchasing/pricing` | Price lookup with the explain table | `GET /pricing/lookup` |
| `/purchasing/catalogs` | Procurement catalogs with a status filter and load-more paging (nav item gated on PurchaseOrderInquire; writes need CatalogMaintain in the service) | `GET /catalogs` |
| `/purchasing/catalogs/new` | Catalog header, validity, operating units and optional first items | `POST /catalogs` |
| `/purchasing/catalogs/:id` | Summary with the item count and items with their images (the first 200, then Show more items a page at a time); Publish or Retire; Import vendor file unless retired; while a draft, add and remove items | `GET /catalogs/{id}`, `GET /catalogs/{id}/entries`, `POST /catalogs/{id}:publish`, `:retire`, `POST /catalogs/{id}/entries`, `DELETE /catalogs/{id}/entries/{entryId}` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/catalog-imports` | Staged vendor files with a status filter and load-more paging | `GET /catalog-imports` |
| `/purchasing/catalogs/:id/import` | Vendor id and a CSV file (at most 25 MB, refused in the browser above it) or pasted rows; stages the file | `POST /catalogs/{id}/vendor-import` |
| `/purchasing/catalog-imports/:id` | Row counts, staged rows with their problems and a status filter; accept or reject a row, accept all rows without errors, add the accepted rows to the catalog, cancel | `GET /catalog-imports/{id}`, `GET /catalog-imports/{id}/rows`, `POST /catalog-imports/{id}/decisions`, `POST /catalog-imports/{id}:commit`, `:cancel` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/requisitions/new` (picker) | Search published catalogs for the legal entity and operating unit, with item images and prices; Add puts a catalog line on the requisition, which sends only `catalogEntryId`, quantity, unit and date; the requisition detail marks each line Catalog or Non-catalog | `GET /catalog-entries` |
| `/purchasing/category-vendors` | Category vendor assignments with status and category filters and load-more paging (nav item gated on PurchaseOrderInquire; writes need CategoryMaintain in the service) | `GET /category-vendors` |
| `/purchasing/category-vendors/new` | Legal entity, category, vendor, status and validity window | `POST /category-vendors` |
| `/purchasing/category-vendors/:id` | The assignment and a change form for status, window and notes; stale version shown with PRC-CAT-013 | `GET /category-vendors/{id}`, `PUT /category-vendors/{id}` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/category-managers` | Category managers with a category filter, an add form and Remove | `GET /category-managers`, `POST /category-managers`, `DELETE /category-managers/{id}` |
| `/purchasing/signing-limits` | Signing limits with document type and expired-only filters, expired limits marked, load-more paging (nav item gated on PurchaseOrderInquire) | `GET /signing-limits` |
| `/purchasing/signing-limits/:id` | One limit and Recertify for 12 months (SigningLimitApprove in the service); stale version shown with PRC-SL-005 | `GET /signing-limits/{id}`, `POST /signing-limits/{id}:recertify` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/signing-limit-requests` | Requests, pending first, with a status filter | `GET /signing-limit-requests` |
| `/purchasing/signing-limit-requests/new` | Legal entity, worker, document, currency, spending and approval limits, reason | `POST /signing-limit-requests` |
| `/purchasing/signing-limit-requests/:id` | The request and, while pending, Approve and Reject with a note (SigningLimitApprove in the service); PRC-SL-003, -004 and -005 shown with their codes | `GET /signing-limit-requests/{id}`, `POST /signing-limit-requests/{id}:approve`, `:reject` (If-Match) |
| `/purchasing/policies` | Saved purchasing policies per legal entity (change management default, PO four-eyes, invoice before receipt, category access), open any legal entity by id | `GET /purchasing-policies` |
| `/purchasing/policies/:legalEntityId` | The settings with their meaning, including the category access mode (hard, soft, off); Save policy (unsaved entity, If-Match 0) or Update policy; stale version shown with PRC-POL-003 | `GET /purchasing-policies/{legalEntityId}`, `PUT /purchasing-policies/{legalEntityId}` (If-Match) |

Pages use the shell primitives from `src/shell/ui` and `pages.module.css` tokens only.
Every data page has loading, empty and error states; errors show the service's stable
problem code. Amounts stay decimal strings from the service and are never parsed to
floats. Ids are typed as UUIDs until the vendor, product and legal-entity pickers from
waves 6 to 8 are available to modules.

Tests: `npx vitest --run src/modules/purchasing` (`pages.test.tsx`, `agreements.test.tsx`,
`consolidation.test.tsx`, `orderLifecycle.test.tsx`, `rfq.test.tsx`, `rfqSealed.test.tsx`, `invoicingPlan.test.tsx`, `reprice.test.tsx`, `scheduling.test.tsx` and `signing.test.tsx` drive every route against a stubbed `fetch` through
`testHarness.tsx`).
